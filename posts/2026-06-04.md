title: The creative block is your creative brief
---

<p><a href="https://www.creativepeptalk.com/start-do-finish-get-around-whats-blocking-you-from-doing-stuff/">Creative Pep Talk (558) - Start, Do, Finish: Get Around What's Blocking You from Doing Stuff</a></p>

<blockquote>[T]he only way to get what you're looking for is to embrace the block, is to go into the block, is to face the block. And you hear people say it like this: The bumps in the road, that is the road. The road is just bumpy. You hear stoics say, the obstacle is the way.</blockquote>

<blockquote>The block <em>is</em> the brief.</blockquote>
